Footstep Energy Generation

ISMART 2026 — Innovation Prototype

Kinetic Harvesting via Piezoelectric Array

A novel prototype that converts mechanical energy from human footsteps into usable electrical energy using a piezoelectric and electromagnetic sensor array. The system tracks active kinetic harvesting via a real-time digital voltage step-counter display, demonstrating how high-footfall environments — corridors, stations, pavements — can become distributed clean energy generators.
